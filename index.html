Benefits you can get
from joining LSPD:

Great medical and dental benefits

You will get quality health services or excellence in the medical field, and improve care and health.

Variety of job opportunities

Job opportunities give you the option to explore various fields and industries. This allows you to choose a career that suits your interests, skills and goals.

Exceptionals pension plans

By offering better, extraordinary retirement plans can increase participant satisfaction. This can strengthen employee loyalty to the company and create a positive image for company in eyes of employees.

Paid military leave

Financial Support getting paid during military leave, employees can focus completely on their duties and responsibilities in military without having to worry about their personal finances or family needs.

Full salary & Benefits while you train

Rewarding Performance full salary can be considered as a reward for good performance and contribution to the job. This can be a motivation for employees to continue to provide good results.
